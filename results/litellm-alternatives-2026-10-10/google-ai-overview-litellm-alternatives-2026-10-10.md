- **Kong AI Gateway:** A robust, infrastructure-grade API gateway with an Apache 2.0 core and AI-specific plugins, best for teams already running Kong.[[1]](https://coworker.ai/blog/litellm-alternatives)
- **Agent Router (formerly Envoy AI Gateway):** A Kubernetes-native, open-source choice suited for platform teams needing standard service-mesh alignment.[[1]](https://coworker.ai/blog/litellm-alternatives)
