The top alternatives to [Braintrust](https://www.braintrust.dev/articles/braintrust-alternatives) depend on whether you are looking for open-source self-hosting, lower pricing, or deeper agentic tracing.[[1]](https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/braintrust-alternatives)[[2]](https://www.thecontextcompany.com/compare/braintrust-alternatives)

### Best Open-Source & Self-Hosted Alternatives

- **[Langfuse](https://langfuse.com/compare/braintrust):** The closest direct competitor to Braintrust. It offers MIT-licensed, full-stack self-hosting (ClickHouse), detailed tracing, prompt management, and LLM-as-judge evaluation, though you must build your own CI/CD orchestration layer.[[1]](https://www.braintrust.dev/articles/braintrust-alternatives)[[2]](https://langfuse.com/compare/braintrust)
- **Arize Phoenix:** An open-source, OTel-native (OpenTelemetry) tracing and evaluation platform with deep auto-instrumentation (50+ integrations) and strong agent-tracing capabilities.[[1]](https://arize.com/docs/phoenix/resources/frequently-asked-questions/braintrust-open-source-alternative-llm-evaluation-platform-comparison)[[2]](https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/braintrust-alternatives)
- **Opik by Comet:** An Apache 2.0 open-source platform combining production observability, evaluation, prompt management, and monitoring.[[1]](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/braintrust-930570360/alternatives)[[2]](https://www.morphllm.com/comparisons/braintrust-alternatives)

### Best Cloud & Enterprise Alternatives

- **LangSmith:** Best for teams built heavily on LangChain or LangGraph. It provides detailed step-by-step trace inspection and CI/CD evaluation gates.[[1]](https://inference.net/content/braintrust-alternatives/)[[2]](https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/braintrust-alternatives)[[3]](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/braintrust-930570360/alternatives)
- **Galileo:** Best for continuous production scoring, safety guardrails, and automated evaluation models (like Luna-2) rather than pre-deployment testing.[[1]](https://www.braintrust.dev/articles/arize-ai-alternatives-2026)[[2]](https://www.braintrust.dev/articles/braintrust-alternatives)
- **Datadog LLM Observability:** Best for engineering teams already using Datadog who want to centralize LLM metrics inside their existing dashboard.[[1]](https://www.braintrust.dev/articles/braintrust-alternatives)
