# Google AI Overview for "llm gateway" (US, desktop, English), saved 2026-10-21 18:36 UTC from the DataForSEO SERP response

An **LLM gateway** is `a unified middleware layer that routes, manages, and tracks artificial intelligence requests between your applications and multiple large language model providers` (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google) through a single OpenAI-compatible API interface.[[1]](https://github.com/theopenco/llmgateway)[[2]](https://openrouter.ai/blog/insights/llm-gateway/)

### Core Functions

- **Unified API:** Replaces fragmented, provider-specific SDKs and auth management with one consistent endpoint.[[1]](https://www.merge.dev/blog/llm-gateway)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LEtUH-2Sydg&t=181)
- **Smart Routing & Failover:** Automatically switches traffic to backup models or providers if a rate limit or service outage occurs.[[1]](https://openrouter.ai/blog/insights/llm-gateway/)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LEtUH-2Sydg&t=181)
- **Cost & Caching Analytics:** Tracks token usage, enforces team spend caps, and caches frequent queries to cut operational costs.[[1]](https://llmgateway.io/)[[2]](https://openrouter.ai/blog/insights/llm-gateway/)
- **Security & Guardrails:** Filters sensitive data, blocks prompt injections, and manages team-level access control.[[1]](https://llmgateway.io/)[[2]](https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/llm-gateway)

### Popular Platforms

- **[LLM Gateway](https://llmgateway.io/):** An open-source, self-hostable (or cloud-managed) gateway routing 250+ models across 40+ providers with bring-your-own-keys (BYOK) or prepaid options.[[1]](https://llmgateway.io/)[[2]](https://docs.llmgateway.io/)
- **LiteLLM / Bifrost:** Lightweight or quick-setup options ideal for Python apps and simple dashboards.[[1]](https://agenta.ai/blog/top-llm-gateways)
- **Kong / Portkey:** Enterprise-grade alternatives focused on deep observability, governance, and security compliance.[[1]](https://portkey.ai/blog/what-is-an-llm-gateway/)[[2]](https://agenta.ai/blog/top-llm-gateways)
