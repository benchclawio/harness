Cursor AI offers a free **Hobby** tier and paid plans starting at **$20/month** on Pro, scaling up to **$60/month** for Pro+, **$200/month** for Ultra, **$40/user/month** for Teams, and custom pricing for Enterprise . ### Cursor Pricing Tiers

- **Pro:** $20/month ($16/month if billed annually). Includes unlimited Auto mode, unlimited Tab completions, and an included pool of frontier-model credits.[[1]](https://amnic.com/blogs/cursor-pricing)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOOq9wzLChg&vl=en-US&t=23)
- **Pro+:** $60/month ($48/month if billed annually). Triples the agent and usage limits of the standard Pro tier.[[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOOq9wzLChg&vl=en-US&t=23)
- **Ultra:** $200/month ($160/month if billed annually). Multiplies Pro limits by roughly 20x for heavy power users and large codebases.[[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOOq9wzLChg&vl=en-US&t=23)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUXDXFe8nq0)
- **Teams:** $40 per user/month. Built for collaborative teams needing shared admin controls and team usage policies.[[1]](https://www.jetadmin.io/blog/cursor-pricing-explained-plans-credit-system-and-real-costs-in-2026/)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOOq9wzLChg&vl=en-US&t=23)
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing. Tailored security, single sign-on (SSO), and custom invoicing requirements.[[1]](https://cursor.com/pricing)[[2]](https://www.superblocks.com/blog/cursor-enterprise)

### How the Credit System Works

- **Included Credits:** Your monthly subscription fee provides a matching budget of usage credits for third-party frontier models (like Claude or GPT series).[[1]](https://www.superblocks.com/blog/cursor-enterprise)[[2]](https://amnic.com/blogs/cursor-pricing)
- **Auto Mode:** Using Cursor's built-in **Auto mode** routes requests through cost-efficient models without rapidly burning down your primary credit pool.[[1]](https://www.finout.io/blog/what-happened-to-cursor-pricing-2026-guide-5-cost-cutting-tips)[[2]](https://amnic.com/blogs/cursor-pricing)
- **Overage Billing:** Once your monthly credit pool is exhausted, additional requests automatically transition to pay-as-you-go metered billing at standard API rates.[[1]](https://flexprice.io/blog/cursor-pricing-guide)[[2]](https://amnic.com/blogs/cursor-pricing)
