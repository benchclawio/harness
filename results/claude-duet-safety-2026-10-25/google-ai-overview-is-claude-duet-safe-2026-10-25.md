### Safety Features

- **Constitutional AI & Safeguards:** Built on Anthropic's safety framework, which uses reinforcement learning to refuse malicious instructions and screen actions.

- **Isolated Environment:** Runs tasks in isolated cloud environments separate from your local network.[[1]](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13364135-use-claude-cowork-safely)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtt89WjpfRM&t=1)[[3]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XMg5kmHkGxc&t=554)

### Key Risks & Concerns
