# Google AI Overview for 'braintrust pricing' (saved from DataForSEO SERP, US)

Braintrust offers three main pricing plans for its AI evaluation and observability platform ([Pricing - Braintrust]):

### **Pricing Plans & Platform Fees** 

- **Starter** ($0 / month): Best for individual developers and small teams to test and build. Includes $10 in monthly model credits, 1 GB of processed data per month, 10,000 scores per month, and 14-day data retention.
- **Pro** ($249 / month): Best for growing production teams. Includes $100 in monthly model credits, 5 GB of processed data per month, 50,000 scores per month, and 30-day data retention.
- **Enterprise** (Custom pricing): Best for large organizations requiring custom limits, RBAC, high-volume/privacy-sensitive data, on-prem or hosted deployments, and a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for HIPAA compliance.



---

### **On-Demand Overage Rates** 
Once you exceed your monthly included allowances, pay-as-you-go rates apply:

- **Processed Data:** 
	- Starter: +$4 per GB after 1 GB
	- Pro: +$3 per GB after 5 GB
- **Scores (LLM-as-a-judge / custom code scorers):** 
	- Starter: $2.50 per 1,000 scores after 10,000
	- Pro: $1.50 per 1,000 scores after 50,000
- **Data Retention Extension:** $0.50 per GB per month for extended storage beyond the base retention period.
- **Model Credits / Token Rates:** Monthly credits cover built-in models and features like Topics; additional usage is billed at specific token-per-million (MTok) rates once credits run out.
