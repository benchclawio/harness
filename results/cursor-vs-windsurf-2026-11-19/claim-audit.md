## Findings and boundaries
- Cursor pricing and models/pricing records list Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100 and Ultra $200 per month. Plus excludes third-party models; prepaid third-party usage starts separately on eligible higher plans. Legacy customer plans differ.
- Devin pricing lists Pro $20, Max $200, and Teams $80/month plus $40 per full development seat. Its billing documentation describes the $80 as shared prepaid credits but does not explain the pricing page's full-seat charge. Do not infer a complete team invoice or a task-cost winner.
- Cursor's download page labels 3.30 Latest; the read did not establish that build's release date.
- Devin's current release list identifies 3.14.30825 on November 18, superseding the November 17 build in the initial article draft. Changelog evidence for Electron 43.8.8 / Node 24.22.1 refers to 3.12.57, November 12, not an installed-build inspection.
- Cognition's June 2 rename announcement introduces Devin Desktop / Devin Local. The current FAQ places the legacy Windsurf JetBrains plugin in maintenance mode. A downloadable plugin does not establish active compatibility.
- Cursor Agent and Devin Local capabilities are vendor descriptions, not exercised behavior. ACP, permissions, sandboxing, migration and billing need deployment-specific verification.
- OX Security's October 21, 2025 Chromium disclosure is historical, not proof of a current vulnerability or present-day safety.
- Cursor ownership was checked against its own joining-SpaceX announcement; no ownership inference was drawn from search snippets.
- The article uses public records, not editor benchmark scores. No speed, generated-code quality, token-efficiency or security winner is claimed.
