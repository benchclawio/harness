The top open-source alternatives to LangGraph for building stateful and multi-agent AI workflows are [CrewAI](https://www.crewai.com/), [Microsoft AutoGen](https://microsoft.github.io/autogen/), PydanticAI , and [LlamaIndex Workflows](https://www.llamaindex.ai/).

### Top Alternatives

- **CrewAI** : Best for structured, role-based multi-agent collaboration where specialized teams of agents execute clearly defined tasks. Unlike LangGraph's low-level graph and state management, CrewAI abstracts teamwork into predefined roles, goals, and tools.
- **Microsoft AutoGen** : Best for complex, conversational multi-agent systems where agents dynamically chat with each other to solve a problem. It excels in flexible agent-to-agent communication, though it has a steeper learning curve and is traditionally more research-oriented than LangGraph.
- **PydanticAI** : Best for Python developers who want a lightweight, type-safe, and production-ready framework without heavy graph abstractions. It leverages Pydantic for structured validation of inputs and outputs during agent runs.
- **LlamaIndex Workflows** : Best if your application is heavily centered around RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and data ingestion. It provides event-driven, step-based orchestration that handles state transitions cleanly without a massive framework footprint.

### How to Choose

- Choose **CrewAI** if you need an out-of-the-box hierarchical or sequential team of specialized agents.
- Choose **AutoGen** if you want autonomous, conversational multi-agent interactions.
- Choose **PydanticAI** if you prefer clean code, strong typing, and minimal overhead.
- Choose **LlamaIndex** if your core logic revolves around searching and indexing private data stores.
