- **LlamaIndex:** Best for data-heavy and RAG-focused applications, featuring over 150 data connectors via LlamaHub and specialized indexing tools.
- **Haystack:** Best for production-ready search and RAG pipelines with strong Kubernetes and scaling support.
- **PydanticAI:** Best for type-safe, model-agnostic agent workflows with clean debugging features.
- **AutoGen:** Best for open-source multi-agent collaboration and conversational agent topologies.
- **CrewAI:** Best for role-based, team-oriented multi-agent prototyping.
- **DSPy:** Best for programmatic prompt optimization rather than manual prompt engineering.[[1]](https://www.langchain.com/resources/langchain-alternatives)[[2]](https://pecollective.com/tools/langchain-alternatives/)[[3]](https://www.lindy.ai/blog/langchain-alternatives)[[4]](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/GvcmEKU8YG8)
